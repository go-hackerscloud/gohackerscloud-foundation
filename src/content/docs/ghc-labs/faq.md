## Is the Foundation free?

Yes, Foundation's publicly accessible educational lessons and learning resources are free to read. Academy memberships and hosted lab access are separate offerings.

## Does every Academy track include a live lab?

Not necessarily. The 13-track directory includes practical tracks, learning resources and challenges. Read individual Academy lesson pages to see whether a specific environment is provisioned and what access is required.

## Do I need an expensive laptop?

Many supported Academy labs are browser-accessible and preconfigured, reducing local setup requirements. You still need a suitable device, stable connection and a modern browser; some advanced or third-party exercises may have separate needs. Check the specific lesson.

## Can I practice penetration testing against any website?

No. Only test systems you own or are explicitly authorized to assess. Academy labs should be used strictly inside their defined targets and exercise scope.

## Are laboratories permanent?

Do not assume that. Hosted practice environments are commonly temporary, and cleanup may remove changes made inside a session. Save permitted notes and evidence outside the lab.

## What will I learn?

Depending on the track: networking and operating systems, web/API assessment, cloud security, IAM, container behavior, DevSecOps, safe investigation methods, professional reporting or project work. No single course guarantees mastery.

## Where do I watch the demonstration?

The [GHC Labs overview](/ghc-labs/) has a reserved video section. A placeholder is shown until an official recording is attached.

## Where do I see current prices and access terms?

Visit the official [Hands-On Cybersecurity Labs page](https://gohackerscloud.com/hands-on-cybersecurity-labs) or [Academy membership page](https://gohackerscloud.com/join-academy). Prices, taxes, refunds, eligibility and renewals should be verified at checkout rather than copied from an older guide.

## Will completing the labs award a professional certification?

An Academy exercise is not itself an industry certification. Follow the certification body's official requirements for any exam or credential.

## What if I cannot launch a lab?

Check access eligibility, the selected lesson's availability, browser session state and whether a previous practice session remains active. Follow the official support process and share only sanitized diagnostic details. Never share cookies, launch tokens or passwords.

## What should I submit after a lab?

Aim for a concise report with scope, objective, observations, interpretation, remediation guidance and retest plan. Distinguish real observations from hypothetical example outputs.
