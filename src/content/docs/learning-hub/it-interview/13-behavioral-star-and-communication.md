[Handbook contents](/learning-hub/it-interview/)

## Core technical and professional concepts

### Fundamental principle

STAR means Situation, Task, Action and Result; focus on what you personally did.

### Practical distinctions

Explain disagreements by describing evidence, constraints and the path to a decision.

### How teams use it

Discuss mistakes candidly, including corrective and preventive steps.

### How to show evidence

When uncertain, say what is known and what test would reduce uncertainty.

## Real interview scenario

A stakeholder asks you to skip deployment review. You acknowledge urgency, describe release risk and identify the approved emergency procedure.

**Answer structure:** restate the requirement; identify scope, constraints and risk; choose a test or decision; verify evidence; communicate the outcome.

## Guided preparation exercise

1. Draft a 90-second STAR story about technical difficulty.
2. Prepare a collaboration example.
3. Prepare an honest mistake-and-learning story.
4. Record and review one explanation for clarity.

Use only your own equipment, public documentation and authorized or synthetic environments. If you have only studied a technology, describe it accurately as study experience.

## Deliverable

Three role-relevant STAR stories.

In a short write-up, include what you know, what you tested, what you inferred and what you could not verify.
