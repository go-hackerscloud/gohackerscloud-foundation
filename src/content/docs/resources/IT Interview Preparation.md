Technical interviews test more than definitions.

Employers want to understand whether you can **apply your knowledge to real-world situations**.

## Prepare Across Four Areas

### Technical Knowledge

Review the fundamentals relevant to your target role.

### Troubleshooting

Practice explaining how you would investigate and resolve technical problems.

### Scenario Questions

Prepare for questions such as:

> A production application suddenly becomes unavailable. How would you begin troubleshooting?

### Communication

Practice explaining technical concepts clearly and logically.

## Build Evidence of Your Skills

Where possible, support your interview preparation with:

- Hands-on labs
- GitHub projects
- Architecture diagrams
- Troubleshooting exercises
- Documentation
- Certification knowledge

## Remember

Don't prepare only to answer interview questions.

**Prepare to demonstrate that you can do the job.**
