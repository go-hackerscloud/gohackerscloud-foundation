# Suggested Weekly Schedule

Consistency is more important than long study sessions.

| Day | Activity |
|------|----------|
| Monday | Learn a new lesson |
| Tuesday | Practice concepts |
| Wednesday | Complete a hands-on lab |
| Thursday | Read documentation and take notes |

# Build Healthy Learning Habits

Success comes from consistency.

Try to:

- Study at the same time each day.
- Remove distractions.
- Take regular breaks.
- Practice more than you watch.
- Review your notes every week.
- Celebrate milestones.
- Stay curious.

Remember:

> **Consistency beats intensity.**
