## Learn by doing — from your first lesson

Foundation is designed for understanding, not copying commands without context. You can complete meaningful beginner exercises on paper, in a notebook or on a computer you own.

| Learn a concept | Try a safe, useful exercise | Keep your evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **IT Fundamentals** | Label CPU, memory, operating system and storage in a system diagram | One labeled system architecture |
| **Networking** | Draw DNS → TCP/TLS → web application and explain each boundary | A simple request-flow diagram |
| **Cloud & Cybersecurity** | Identify a business asset, a possible risk and an appropriate security control | An asset–risk–control worksheet |

**Try your first free lesson:** [Computer Hardware](/foundation/gohackerscloud-foundation/01-it-fundamentals/01-computer-hardware/) · [Networking Basics](/foundation/gohackerscloud-foundation/02-networking-fundamentals/01-networking-basics/) · [Security Mindset](/foundation/gohackerscloud-foundation/08-cybersecurity-fundamentals/01-security-mindset/)

## Build a stronger foundation with a clear plan

The [Core IT & DevOps deep-dive](/core-it-devops/) connects foundational concepts to practical work: systems, networking, Linux, Windows, Python and Git. Follow the [12-week study plan](/study-plan/) at your own pace or begin with the [student pathway](/paths/students/) and [professional pathway](/paths/professionals/).

Every lesson should help you answer three questions: *What does this do? Why would an organization use it? How would I verify it safely?*
