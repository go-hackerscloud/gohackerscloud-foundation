## How to learn with Foundation

1. **Learn:** Read the lesson, check its learning objectives and build a glossary.
2. **Practise:** Try the suggested safe exercises on your own computer or in a permitted local practice environment.
3. **Check understanding:** Use the module reviews and assessments to identify gaps.
4. **Keep progressing:** Summarize what you learned and move to the next module.

[Browse every module](/curriculum/) · [Follow a 12-week study plan](/study-plan/) · [Read the beginner guide](/start-here/)

## Free Foundation vs. advanced Academy

**Foundation is free:** you can read its published lessons and use its study guides without a paid membership. Practice suggestions can be completed independently with your own tools.

**Academy and GoHackersCloud Labs are separate:** advanced learning tracks, hosted hands-on labs, progress dashboards and related membership features have their own access terms. Explore [GoHackersCloud Academy](https://academy.gohackerscloud.com/) when you are ready for guided specialization; no purchase is needed to use Foundation.
