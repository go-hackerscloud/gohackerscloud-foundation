# What's Next?

➡️ **Module 15 — Enterprise Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC)**

Building secure cloud environments requires more than deploying firewalls, encryption, and Identity & Access Management.

Enterprise organizations must also ensure that their technology, people, and processes comply with business objectives, industry standards, contractual obligations, and regulatory requirements.

This is where **Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC)** becomes essential.

In this module, you'll learn how enterprise organizations establish security governance, manage business and technology risks, implement compliance frameworks, conduct security assessments, perform audits, and build security programs that align with business goals.

Rather than viewing security as only a technical responsibility, you'll understand how governance enables organizations to make informed business decisions while protecting critical assets.

You'll learn about:

- Security Governance
- Enterprise Risk Management
- Compliance Frameworks
- Security Policies & Standards
- Risk Assessments
- Security Audits
- Third-Party Risk Management
- Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery Governance
- Security Metrics & Reporting
- Enterprise GRC Best Practices

By the end of this module, you'll understand how enterprise organizations build, manage, and continuously improve mature Governance, Risk & Compliance programs that support secure cloud and cybersecurity operations.
