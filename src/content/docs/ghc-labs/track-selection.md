## Select by learning outcome

## Choose a role before selecting a track

Use the [nine career roadmaps](/learning-hub/career-paths/) to find out how your target job relates to the Academy's 13 track categories. Each roadmap points to [suggested third-party certification pathways](/learning-hub/certification-paths/) and [role-specific technical interview questions](/learning-hub/interview-paths/). The [five-video lab library](/ghc-labs/instructor-demo/) introduces different practical workflows as official recordings are released.

For example, penetration testing maps to **Network, Web and API** tracks; DevSecOps maps to **AppSec, Cloud and Projects**; SOC analyst practice maps to **CompTIA, Cloud and Projects**. These are learning-track links, not confirmations that a separate hosted lab instance exists for every topic.

## Evidence quality checklist

- **Scope:** authorized lab, target and objective stated clearly.
- **Method:** steps are reproducible and relevant to the objective.
- **Observation:** record what actually happened, not an expected result presented as fact.
- **Interpretation:** explain the control, weakness or behavior shown by the evidence.
- **Remediation:** describe the principle or configuration correction.
- **Verification:** include a retest or a clear plan for one.
- **Ethics:** no testing beyond the lab's authorized scope.

## Teaching progression

Begin with an instructor demonstration, continue with a guided exercise, then repeat independently and submit a short report. The instructor should explain each command's purpose and how the technique maps to work in a security team.

The track directory is a navigation guide, not a live capacity or lab-availability feed. Check each lesson and access terms before promising a particular lab to a student.
