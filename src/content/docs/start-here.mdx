## Welcome to the free Foundation

This is your starting point if you are a college student, an absolute beginner, a working professional refreshing fundamentals or someone considering a technology career.

You **do not need a paid Academy subscription** to read Foundation lessons. Some existing lesson material mentions GoHackersCloud Academy, lab exercises or student dashboards; those refer to separate advanced offerings, not a requirement to learn here.

Before your first lesson, you can read [About GoHackersCloud](/about-gohackerscloud/) for an overview of all our offerings. [Courses](/about-gohackerscloud/courses/), [test preparation](/about-gohackerscloud/test-engine/), [GHC Labs](/ghc-labs/) and [professional services](/about-gohackerscloud/cybersecurity-services/) are explained separately so you can choose the right path without confusing Foundation with a paid product.

**Choose a route for your experience:** [Student & Beginner Learning Path](/paths/students/) · [Working Professional Upskilling Path](/paths/professionals/). Both use free published Foundation lessons; no course or lab subscription is needed.

**Want a practical first week?** Take the [7-Day Free Foundation Challenge](/7-day-challenge/): seven linked lessons, seven safe exercises and 21 original questions. Optional browser-only progress keeps the challenge accessible without creating an account.

## Step 2 — Use a simple learning routine

For each lesson:

1. Read the objective before the explanation.
2. Note 3–5 new words and their meanings.
3. Draw a small diagram or describe how the concept appears at work.
4. Practise only with your own systems, authorized services or safe examples.
5. Answer review questions without looking at the solution first.
6. Write a short summary and choose the next lesson.

A notebook, a browser and basic local tools are enough for many introductory topics. Special-purpose hosted labs are **not** included automatically with Foundation.

## Step 3 — Build one small piece of evidence

Examples:

- IT: label the CPU, memory, storage and operating system in a computer diagram.
- Networking: sketch how DNS, routing and HTTPS connect a browser to a site.
- Linux: list common directories and explain basic file permissions.
- Cloud: compare IaaS, PaaS and SaaS using familiar services.
- Security: identify a risk, affected asset and possible safeguard.
- Professional skills: write a one-page technical summary.

Keep the work in your own notes. These are practice suggestions, not required graded submissions.

## Step 4 — Make steady progress

The [12-week Foundation study plan](/study-plan/) is an *optional pacing guide*, not a promise to master every topic in three months. You can take longer or focus on the modules relevant to your interests.

- **After each lesson:** explain one concept in your own words.
- **After each module:** revisit its review or assessment material.
- **After the Foundation:** explore a career roadmap or choose a more advanced [Academy track](https://academy.gohackerscloud.com/tracks/).
